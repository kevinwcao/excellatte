THE PROBLEM

Finding great coffee shouldn’t require knowing everything about coffee

Generic ratings tell you what everyone likes. Specialty coffee tools often assume you already know exactly what you want. I wanted to explore a simpler experience for people who are still learning their own taste: start with what you actually drink, capture what you liked or disliked, and let those signals make the next recommendation more useful.

THE PRODUCT

Coffee recommendations that get better as they get to know you

Excellatte is an AI-powered coffee discovery experience that learns from what you drink, what you rate, and what you say — then uses those signals to recommend what you might enjoy next.

HOW I SOLVED IT

Your coffee history becomes your recommendation engine

Ratings, written notes, drink history, and recommendation feedback become signals that gradually build a more useful picture of what you enjoy. Recommendations stay flexible: reject one, explain why, change your current mood, or deliberately venture outside your comfort zone.

AI will become a core part of how great product managers work

I believe learning to work effectively with AI will make me a better product manager — helping me explore ideas faster, build more independently, and spend more time on the judgment that matters. As these tools become part of everyday product development, I wanted to start building that relationship now rather than wait for the workflow to become standard.

Excellatte gave me a practical way to learn by building

The product started with a simple question: Could your coffee history help predict what you’ll enjoy next? Building Excellatte gave me a practical way to test that idea while learning where AI genuinely accelerates product work, where it needs direction, and where human judgment still matters most.
